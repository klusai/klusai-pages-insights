## The challenge

Legal professionals run on confidential documents. Before any of them can be
analysed, identifying details must be removed and the data inside has to be
captured. Every research answer a lawyer relies on must trace back to its
source.

DONNAJAMES wanted to do this for hundreds of independent practices at once,
with three non-negotiables: each practice's data kept strictly separate, all
processing inside the European Economic Area, and no client data ever used to
train models. And it had to be live in months, not years.

## Claude at the core

We chose Claude for the work where quality matters most. In the DONNAJAMES
platform, Claude understands the documents, extracts the data they contain,
runs research across Dutch legislation, professional guidance and each
practice's own precedents, and cites every source it uses, so a lawyer can
verify every answer. It also powers the assistant, a library of task-specific
micro-agents, and drafting from the practice's own Word templates.

## Built on kOS, live in weeks

kOS, the EU-sovereign platform we build and run our own products on, already
covered an estimated 70–80% of DONNAJAMES's requirements: multi-tenancy,
hybrid retrieval with reranking, an agentic assistant with MCP support,
document generation and full observability. That head start let a compact team
of an architect, an engineer and a delivery lead go from kickoff to
production-ready in six two-week sprints.

## Early results

The platform went live in September 2026 and onboarding is under way, with
more than 200 legal professionals expected on the platform by October. The
first weeks in production already show the impact: anonymising documents is
approximately 70% faster, and users save around 15% of their working week.
