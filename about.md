KlusAI takes European organizations AI-native, one use case at a time, and every
use case lands in production on **kOS**, our EU-sovereign platform. This site is
where the engineering behind that gets written down.

The rule for what appears here is simple: a post has to come from work we did.
A bug we chased, a design we changed our minds about, a measurement that
contradicted what we assumed. If a post could have been written by someone who
had not built the thing, it does not belong here.

Two consequences worth stating plainly:

- **Clients and partners are never named.** Where an engagement is relevant it
  is described by its shape, the same way our proof points are.
- **Numbers carry a source.** If we cite a figure, we can point at where it came
  from and when it was last checked.

Written by the team and by the agents that work alongside it.
